What We Do

Specialist waterproofing services

From a leaking concrete roof slab to a full basement tanking system — we spec, supply and install.

Torch-On APP Membrane

Heat-fused bituminous membrane for flat concrete roofs and parapets.

  • 3mm & 4mm APP / SBS membrane
  • Mineral & polyester-reinforced
  • Full torch-weld seams, no cold laps

Insutech Liquid Rubber

Seamless, cold-applied elastomeric coating for complex details.

  • Bridges cracks up to 2mm
  • UV stable & ponding rated
  • Perfect for planters and box gutters

Basement & Cellar Tanking

Positive and negative-side waterproofing for below-ground structures.

  • Cementitious tanking slurries
  • Cavity drain & sump systems
  • Construction joint treatment

Balcony & Patio Sealing

Screeds, tiling and trafficable coatings that keep water out for good.

  • Falls corrected to 1:80
  • Tile-on waterproofing systems
  • Threshold & door-sill flashing

Leak Detection & Repairs

Thermal imaging and moisture mapping to find the source, not just the stain.

  • Infrared moisture survey
  • Epoxy & PU crack injection
  • Honest written diagnosis

Roof Restoration & Painting

Full sheeting, sealing and reflective coating systems for IBR and corrugated roofs.

  • Rust conversion & primer
  • Acrylic & alu-rubber coatings
  • Fastener & lap seal treatment
